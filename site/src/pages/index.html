Schedules and Themes

Spa night in one tap.

  • ThemesForce a mode for a set time. Schedules wait until it ends.

Its own network

Works without home internet.

Your internet goes down, your pool doesn't. The controller makes its own Wi-Fi network, so your phone connects straight to it. It can join your home Wi-Fi or use a network cable too. Away from home, reach it through our cloud service or your own Home Assistant, whichever you prefer.

FAQ

Questions owners ask first.

Do I need home internet?

No. The controller broadcasts its own Wi-Fi network and the app connects to it directly. It can also join your home Wi-Fi or use a network cable.

Can I control it when I'm away from home?

Yes, and you choose how. Use the app through our cloud service, or connect through your own Home Assistant and keep remote access on your own setup. There's no subscription and no monthly fee either way.

Can I run things without my phone?

Yes. The controller has five buttons on its faceplate. Give each one a device and switch it right there.

Will it work with my variable-speed pump?

AquaStar and Fairland variable-speed pumps are documented in the user manual. They connect to the controller over RS-485. Not sure about yours? Put the pump model in your quote request.

I already have an automation panel. Do I replace it?

You can upgrade it instead. The legacy automation conversion kit includes a motherboard, screen, wiring harness and transformer for your existing panel. Mention it in your quote request.

Does it work with Apple Watch, Siri, Alexa or Google Home?

Yes, through Home Assistant. Add Control Freak to Home Assistant with the Home Assistant integration, and your pool shows up in Apple Home, on your Apple Watch, with Siri, Alexa and Google Home, wherever your Home Assistant setup already reaches.

Where can I see how setup works?

Setup videos are on the YouTube channel Control Freak Pool Tech.
