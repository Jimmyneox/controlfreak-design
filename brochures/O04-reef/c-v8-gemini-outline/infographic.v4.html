01Wi-Fi drops

When Wi-Fi or a cloud app hiccups, the tank is left flying blind, and pumps and heater still need to do the right thing.

01Under the stand

The controller under the stand runs pumps, heater and schedules on your home network, so a Wi-Fi drop changes nothing.

02Runaway heater

A heater stuck on, or a power cut while you’re away, puts the whole tank at risk.

02Heater cutoff

Two temperature probes cut heater power above your limit. If the controller loses power, return pump and powerheads stay on and the heater turns off.

03Five apps, one tank

Overfills, water on the floor, and juggling five vendor apps just to see how the tank is doing.

03All in one kit

Auto top-off with overfill lockout, a leak sensor under the stand, pH and a 3-head doser (Pro), and Home Assistant pre-installed.
