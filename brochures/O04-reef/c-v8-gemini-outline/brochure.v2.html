ONE APP

How many apps do you have to open to control your smart products? Home Assistant comes pre-installed with the Control Freak integration, so the tank and the rest of the home land in one place. Ask Alexa, Siri, or Google through Home Assistant bridges — and get alerts, including when you’re away (cloud with Tailscale). Home Assistant itself stays on your home network.

WHY IT’S DIFFERENT

Local when the internet drops. The box under the stand runs the tank. A Wi-Fi drop or server outage doesn’t change what pumps and heater do.

Top-off + leak alarm in the kit. Tops up evaporation with an overfill lockout; a sensor under the stand flags water on the floor. Add only a reservoir.

One app for tank and home. Home Assistant pre-installed with the Control Freak integration — the tank joins the rest of the house.
