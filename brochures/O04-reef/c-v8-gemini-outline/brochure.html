ONE APP

How many apps do you open to run your home? Reef Control Pro ships with Home Assistant and the Control Freak integration already set up, so your tank and the rest of your smart home meet in one place. Connect the devices you already own. Simplify to a single app. Alexa, Siri, and Google Home use Home Assistant’s normal bridges. Get alerts, including when you’re away (cloud with Tailscale). Home Assistant stays on your home network — never cloud Home Assistant.

WHY IT’S DIFFERENT

Steady, even offline. The controller under the stand runs the tank. If the Wi-Fi drops, your pumps and heater don’t miss a beat.

Top-off and leak alarm, included. Evaporation replaced, overfill locked out, water on the floor caught early. Just add a reservoir.

One app for the reef and the home. Home Assistant and the Control Freak integration, ready out of the box.
