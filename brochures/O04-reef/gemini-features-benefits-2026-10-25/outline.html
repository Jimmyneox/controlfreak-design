In Perfect Balance

Automatic dosing and pH control work together to keep your tank's environment at its optimal levels continuously. Instead of sudden swings, this constant feedback loop and real-time micro-adjustments provide a much healthier, stress-free environment for your coral and fish to live in.

Proactive Reservoir Monitoring

Auto Water Leveling That Talks to You

Our control system continuously monitors the height of the water and ensures that it stays at an optimal height automatically. When the top-off reservoir finally gets low, we notify you immediately via phone push notifications—or out loud by voice through Alexa or Google Home—so you know exactly when to add water.
