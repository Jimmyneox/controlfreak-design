TERRARIUM CONTROL PRO · WHAT IT DOES

One controller for the whole shelf.

Each heat source holding its own setpoint, lights on a natural day, and misting on time — for 1–3 enclosures, without stacking a separate thermostat per enclosure or juggling vendor apps.

Set up in minutes: scan the QR code; keyed, colour-coded plugs only fit their port. Built on a controller we already ship — the same controller as Control Freak’s live pool product, not a one-off board. Profiles are editable starting points. Price to be announced.

IN THE PRO KIT

  • Controller with Home Assistant pre-installed + Control Freak integration
  • Power Hub — 8 switched outlets, on/off (heat mats, ceramic emitters, UVB, day lights, fans, mister)
  • 4 temperature probes — hot side, cool side, basking spot, second enclosure
  • Door switch + mister reservoir float CONCEPT
Concept accessory: a separate dimming module for heat bulbs — the Power Hub and controller don’t dim. Later: temperature & humidity sensor (with Modbus support), extra probes, a second Power Hub.
