WHY IT MATTERS · 01 · EQUALIZED WEAR

Two pumps that actually work as a team.

Most homes with dual pumps have a primary unit doing all the heavy lifting while a backup pump sits idle for years — often failing the single time it is called upon. Basement Guard Pro manages your existing equipment with equalized wear. It alternates duty weekly, monitors amp draw, and staggers activations so your circuit never trips under dual startup surges. If the lead pump falls behind heavy storm inflow, the second pump smoothly engages to double capacity. Each pump keeps its own factory float.

WHY IT MATTERS · 02 · LOCAL-FIRST · CLOUD-FREE FOUNDATION

Reliability when internet or power fails.

Real storm weather knocks out connectivity and utility power. A basement system that relies on a remote cloud server to make switching decisions is a liability. All logic, pump rotation, high-water thresholds and the indoor siren run locally on the box in your basement. If the hub is unplugged, your pumps run directly on their own floats. When you’re away, status reaches your phone over secure networking — with no monthly fees.

BASEMENT GUARD PRO · WHAT IT DOES

Stop listening for the pump.

Plug the two pumps you already own into the Power Hub, drop in the level sensor, scan the QR code. No rewiring. From then on the pumps take turns, the pit level is a number you can see, and leaks and cold pipes get noticed early — whether you’re upstairs or a state away.

Built for homeowners with a basement sump, second-home and rental owners who can’t pop downstairs, and Home Assistant users who’d rather not build it by hand. Water and freezing claims average $15,400 (Insurance Information Institute) — a little attention goes a long way.

IN THE RECOMMENDED PACKAGE

  • Controller with Home Assistant + Control Freak integration
  • Power Hub — labelled outlets for two sump pumps, heat tape, a dehumidifier and a spare
  • Pit level sensor (depth + rate of rise)
  • High-water float (second check)
  • Leak sensors — water heater + washer
  • Temperature probes — basement, crawlspace, water heater area, outdoor
  • Siren for inside the house
  • Backup inverter hook-up (for one you already own)
  • Keyed, colour-coded cables
Your sump pumps aren’t in the box — keep the ones you have. Every sensor is its own cart line; the full package costs less than buying lines separately. Optional: UPS, partner 12 V battery backup pump.
