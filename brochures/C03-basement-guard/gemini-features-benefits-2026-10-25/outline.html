04 · Hardwired Safety

Pumps work if controller doesn’t

Pump outlets stay continuously powered even if the controller is off, and each pump keeps its own float switch. Your basement is never worse off than with plain pumps.

01

Two pumps that actually work as a team.

Most homes with dual pumps have a primary unit doing all the heavy lifting while a backup pump sits frozen in stagnant water for years — often failing the single time it is called upon.

Basement Guard Pro manages your existing equipment with equalized wear. It alternates duty weekly, monitors amp draw profile, and staggers activations so your electrical circuit never trips under dual startup surges. If primary discharge capacity falls behind high storm inflow, the secondary pump smoothly engages to double capacity.

Maintains full factory mechanical float independence
02

Reliability when internet or power fails.

Real storm weather knocks out connectivity and utility power. A basement defense system that relies on a remote cloud server to make switching decisions is a liability.

All logic, pump rotation rules, high-water thresholds, and indoor siren alerts run locally on the physical box inside your basement. If the hub is unplugged, your pumps run directly on their own floats. When you leave home, remote status reaches your phone cleanly via secure networking — with zero mandatory recurring monthly fees.

Local-first execution · Cloud-free foundation
