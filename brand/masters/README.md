## Motif grammar (keep when making product marks)

1. Rounded rectangular outer stroke (PCB board outline).
2. Broken corner traces ending in circular pads (vias).
3. Center wordmark (parent: CONTROL / FREAK; product: product short name).
4. Top motif (parent: ladder into a pool capsule; product: that line’s icon).
5. Bottom motif (parent: water wave; product: optional ground/water cue).

Do not stretch, recolor the metallic hero, or drop the PCB pads.
