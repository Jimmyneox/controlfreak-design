# Lockup packs — "brought to you by Control Freak" (v1, per PARENT_BRAND.md v1.2 §5)

Parent attribution group: `brought to you by` (Inter 500, sentence case) + parent line mark.
Product teams place their own product mark above (stacked) or left of (horizontal) this group; never between "by" and the mark.

| Variant | Mark ink | Attribution | Plate |
|---|---|---|---|
| light | forest #0f2e2c | slate #6F8BAB | cream #efe9df (-on-plate) or transparent |
| dark | cream #efe9df | cream-tint #d9d2c6 | forest #0f2e2c (-on-plate) or transparent |
| dark-gold | gold #a8834f (sparingly) | cream-tint | forest |
| currentColor | inherits | inherits | none (SVG only) |

Clear space = 1/8 mark width on all sides. Keep parent mark >= 20 px tall when the attribution line is shown.
No metallic hero, no navy/aqua/coral. PNGs in png/ at @1x and @2x. Generated by ../build_lockups.py from brand/masters/control-freak-mark-currentColor.svg.
